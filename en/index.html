Privacy on this website

These pages are served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you visit, GitHub processes technical access data (IP address, date and time, requested address, referrer, browser identifier) and, according to GitHub, logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. GitHub processes this data under its own responsibility according to its privacy statement, which also governs the retention period; we have no access to these logs. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing these texts securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, so transfers to the USA are covered by the European Commission's adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR). This website sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser, uses no JavaScript, loads no content from other servers and uses no audience measurement. The controller is Lukas Körber (contact in the legal notice); your rights and the competent supervisory authority are listed in the privacy policies linked above.
