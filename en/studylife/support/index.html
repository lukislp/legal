Frequently asked questions

What do I need to use the app?

A StudyAtrium server of your own (or one run by someone who gave you access). There is no central StudyAtrium service and no account with the publisher. On first launch you enter the address of your server and sign in there; the app connects only to that server (and to Apple for notifications). Without a server the app cannot be used. The setup guide explains how to set one up.

Can I try it without my own server?

Yes: enter studylife-demo.lktec.org as the server address. It is a public, read-only demo with sample data; changes, registration and account deletion are switched off. Details are in section 6 of the privacy policy.

Where is my data and who can see it?

On your server and, as a cache, on your device. The operator of the server is responsible for it, the publisher of the app receives none of it. See the privacy policy.

Do I get push notifications with a self-hosted server?

Not from your server. Apple only accepts push messages for this app that are signed with a key held by the publisher, and that key is deployed only on the instance used for Apple's App Review. A server that you run yourself therefore cannot send push notifications or remote Live Activity updates to the App Store version of the app. This is a limit of Apple's push system, not a setting you missed.

What you do get with a self-hosted server:

  • Reminders that the app schedules itself on your device (for example for planned study sessions), also without a connection to the server, as long as you allowed notifications.
  • Live Activities, widgets, shortcuts and the Apple Watch app, which show the state of the app as of its last contact with your server. The Live Activity of the study timer updates while the app is open or in the background, but not through a remote push while the app is closed.
  • Browser notifications, if you use the web interface of your server in a browser: they go through your browser's own push service and do not depend on Apple's key for the app.

Reports, nudges and other messages that your server would send as push notifications reach you in the app when you open it, not as a push message on the lock screen.

Which server version do I need?

Keep your server up to date. A newer app can use functions that an old server does not offer yet. The release notes on GitHub list what changed.

What happens to my data on the device when I sign out or delete my account?

The app removes its local copies: caches, widget and Apple Watch data, Spotlight entries, exported files, shared text not yet sent and delivered notifications. Section 3.3 of the privacy policy lists what is removed. To delete the account itself, use Setup in the app (section 5.1).
